# Bleak Falls Barrow — reference notes

Sources: UESP pages (saved text in docs/maps/ref/bfb/web/), the mirhayasu map
(bfb_overview.png + grid maps), UESP local maps (uesp_map_temple/sanctum.png),
screenshots (ext_barrow, int_bandit_camp, int_claw_door, int_wordwall_*).

## Layout (overview map numbering)
Cells in the game: BARROW (entry, rooms 1-2), TEMPLE (rooms 3-9), SANCTUM (10-12).
1  Entrance from outside (big double doors on the terrace).
2  Entry chamber — 2 bandits by a campfire (spit, bedrolls, chest, chopping block,
   urns, dead skeever, fallen pillars, upright carved coffin lids). Long passage
   west with stairs, side door pairs, then up stairs to 3.
3  Pillar puzzle room — 3 rotating pillars (answer Snake, Snake, Whale, left to
   right; the middle hint has fallen to the floor). Wrong lever = dart trap from
   the floor grate (kills the bandit). Thief skill book + chest on the upper
   level. Gate opens; spiral stairs down — skeevers at the bottom.
4  Spider lair — webs, eggs, the wounded Frostbite Spider drops from the ceiling,
   Arvel stuck in a web at the far end.
5  South burial passage — long hall with wall graves (draugr wake), pressure
   plate + spike wall trap. Arvel dies here; drops Golden Claw + Arvel's Journal.
6  North burial passage — swinging blades (chain past them stops them), oil pools
   with hanging lanterns (shoot to ignite under draugr).
7  Waterfall cavern — chest; pull wall chain to open the gate.
8  Sunlit cavern — stream loop, chest at the end of the water; go back up.
9  Sanctum access — strong draugr, chest; door to the Sanctum.
10 Sanctum's first chamber — swinging blades corridor, patrolling draugr, more
   draugr; the claw door (Hall of Stories): rings Bear (outer), Moth, Owl (inner),
   claw inserted in the centre.
11 Main chamber — huge cave, raised stone platform with steps on the sides, two
   fire braziers, the Overlord's sarcophagus, curved word wall (FUS) with a
   dragon-head carving, two waterfalls, stairs up on the left; big chest.
   Draugr Overlord rises when you learn the word; drops the Dragonstone.
12 Exit tunnel winding to the outside (a shortcut down the mountain).

## Exterior (ext_barrow.png, overview sketch)
Built against the mountain north-west of Riverwood (south of Whiterun). Bottom up:
two lone stone arches on the snow slope; broken stairs to a wide stone platform
with a parapet; a second flight up to the entrance terrace; rows of tall leaning
stone ribs meeting in pointed arches, each topped with a carved bird head; fallen
blocks, a lone snowy pine, the rocky peak behind. Ruined watchtower (Bleak Falls
Tower, 3 bandits, chest at top) on the path below. Bandits on the terrace.

## Arvel's Journal (book text)
My fingers are trembling. The Golden Claw is finally in my hands, and with it,
the power of the ancient Nordic heroes. That fool Lucan Valerius had no idea that
his favorite store decoration was actually the key to Bleak Falls Barrow.
Now I just need to get to the Hall of Stories and unlock the door. The legend
says there is a test that the Nords put in place to keep the unworthy away, but
that "When you have the golden claw, the solution is in the palm of your hands."

## Traps (UESP, damage at player level 1-10)
- Pressure plate: round stone with a Nordic rune, clack sound; cannot be disarmed,
  avoid by walking round it; triggers darts, spike walls, rams.
- Dart trap: tubes in wall/floor; volley of darts, 1 damage per dart (generic).
- Spike wall: spiked grate on a wall hinge, swings out then resets; 75 damage,
  knocks back.
- Swinging blades: pendulum blades in wall channels, swing until a pull chain
  past them is used; 30 damage per hit.
- Oil slick + oil lanterns: harmless until lit (fire spell, torch, shot lantern);
  burns several seconds, 10 fire damage per second; oil burns away.
- Pull chain: wall chain with a ring; opens gates or stops traps.
- Battering ram (log on ceiling bracket): pressure plate/tripwire; small area.

## Extra details (Multicursal article + screenshots in ref/bfb/mc, ext_concept_art, ext_barrow_tower)
- Exterior: the ruined round watchtower (Bleak Falls Tower) stands on a crag
  right of / below the barrow. Concept art: wide terrace paved in flagstones with
  a round well-like pit in the middle, bird-headed stone posts along the edge,
  a broad stairway down the front, the arched façade on a raised plinth behind.
- Entry chamber: huge, half-collapsed hall, light shafts from holes in the roof,
  broken arches, rubble; dead skeevers and a dead bandit on the floor as warning.
- Pillar room: three pillars in arched wall niches (snake, snake, whale), matching
  carved plaques above the iron-barred gate, lever in front of the gate; upper
  balcony with stairs at the right; floor dart holes; a bandit pulls the wrong
  lever and dies.
- After the gate: treasure, small stair down; skeevers come up the stairs.
- Web room before the spider: table with two candlesticks, Scroll of Fireball and
  a paralysis poison (frostbite spiders are weak to fire).
- South burial passage: thick carved pillars, wall coffins in niches, an iron
  portcullis, a round rune pressure plate on the floor (well lit), swinging wall.
- Blade corridor: a run of round arches, blades swing in time; pull chain with a
  ring on the wall past them.
- Hanging oil pots in the hallways — knock them down onto oil to burn enemies.
- Cave section: stream bed, green moss, light from openings, water leads the way.
- Sanctum first room: big hall crossed by a stone bridge with oil pots hanging
  from it, round grate in the floor, swinging door trap at the entry, draugr
  archers; potion of resist cold on a side table.
- Claw door set in a deep arched alcove at the end of a hallway.
- Overlord's weapon always carries a frost enchantment.

## Interior look (v0.4.20, from the Multicursal screenshots in ref/bfb/mc)
- Warm ochre stone of big pillowed blocks (texture TX_NORDIC, a 2 m tile), some carved with swirls; worn,
  wavy joints; flagstone floors of the same stone. Caves: grey rock with cracks.
- Walls rise straight to ~69 % of the height, then a two-facet haunch curves in up to 110 cm over the room:
  passages read as low vaults. Passages lowered to 3.4 m.
- Heavy round arches (IN_ARCH) over the passages and the blades' gallery; the Hall of Stories (sanctum,
  past the claw door) is a ribbed vault with murals (IN_MURAL) on both walls.
- Round swelling columns (IN_NORCOL) replace the square pillars.
- Puzzle pillars: three-sided steles on round feet, snake / bird / whale reliefs; they grind round a third of
  a turn when used. The answer above the gate: carved stone heads with the animal on the tablet under the chin.
- Fandom (The Golden Claw) adds, not yet built: a handle at the top of the stairs left of the word wall opens
  the one-way passage out; the last room has a chest and mountain flowers.
